Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The Kinky List is a private, shared wish list for adults. What you put in it is intimate, so we collect as little as we can and never sell or share it for advertising.

What we store

  • Your account: username, display name, chosen color, your password (stored only as a secure hash, never readable), and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Your spaces: which spaces you belong to and your role in them.
  • What you add and play: wishes, categories, truths, dares and questions; what was drawn, who it was given to, your answers (Interested, Maybe eventually, Not for me), and the memory notes you write when you check something off. Each has a date and time.

We don't ask for your email address, phone number, real name, location, contacts or photos.

Who can see it

Only the members of a space can see that space's lists, to-dos, answers and memories. A space only gains members when someone enters its invite code. Nothing you write is public.

Cookies and tracking

We use two cookies that the app needs to work: one keeps you logged in, and one protects forms from being submitted by other websites. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers.

Services we rely on

  • Hosting: the app and its database run on a hosting provider's servers, which store your data on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts: the app's typefaces load from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when they load, as with any web request. Google doesn't receive anything you write in the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and answers are deleted right away. Any space where you were the only member is deleted along with its lists, to-dos and memories. In spaces you share, the list stays for the other members, and anything you added is kept without your name. Deleted data may remain in the host's routine backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your choices

  • Edit your display name and color on your profile.
  • Leave any space from its settings page.
  • Delete your account at any time{% if user.is_authenticated %} from Profile → Delete account{% else %} from Profile → Delete account after logging in{% endif %}.

Adults only

The Kinky List is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly allow anyone younger to use it. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
